## Standing rules

- 1. (rev 11-03) Plan A is a STOP entry 2-5 past the prior session's extreme (or tested level) in the plan direction; rally-fade/dip-buy LIMITs only as Plan B, max 15 from price_ref at a level tested in the last session. (A limits 10-28 to 11-02: 0-for-10, missed by 0.7-50.)
- 2. Plan B stop entry just past the prior session's extreme (prior day low/high), not a further round number. (10-29: stop 4,020 under the 4,024 low filled; r2 3,996 under 4,000 did not.)
- 3. (rev 11-05) After a day move > 2.5% with H1 RSI < 20 (or > 80) - or > 2.5% alone when H1 RSI is not available - do not add new stop entries in that direction for the next session; Plan A may then be a retest LIMIT at the broken level (overrides rule 1 for that session); Plan B becomes the reclaim/counter setup at the broken level. The 'next session' is the first one after that day closes (11-23 clarification). (10-30 r2 sells 90 lower after -3%: nothing filled; 11-04 r2 buy stop 4,069 after +3.3% filled ~1 below the day's top, RSI unknown.)
- 4. (11-06) Stop entries max 25 from price_ref; if the session extreme is farther, use the nearest tested level within 25 or skip it. (Stops 27-50 away missed on 10-28, 11-02, 11-03 r0, 11-05 r1/r2 4,151 by 3 after a 30+ pullback.)
- 5. (rev 11-10) Put TP1/TP2 3-5 inside the nearest tested extreme or level (before it), never at or past it - including the current session's own high/low once it is printed; a TP may sit past it only as TP2. (11-05 TP2 4,155 vs high 4,148; 11-06 TP1 4,030 vs low 4,033.29; 11-10 r1 TP1 4,004 vs low 4,012.70, missed by 8.7.)
- 6. (11-12) SL at least 5 past the level it guards and at least 1/3 of the prior day range from entry. (11-03 limit SL 15 = 1/5 of range, hit; 11-11 r0 SL 1.2 past 4,067.8 survived by 1.1.)
- 7. (rev 11-23) Entries max 10 from price_ref, built only from a high/low printed within the last 1 h, when (a) the session follows a > 2.5% day, or (b) a revision is made after a high-impact US release has printed (or Friday after 12:00 ET); if that is not possible, post no entry for that side. Exception: a stop 3-5 past the current session's own extreme in the bias direction may be up to 20 from ref. (Misses: 10-30 r2, 11-06 r1, 11-12, 11-13 r2, 11-17 r2. Exception: 11-19 r2 stop 13.5 off would have run ~58; 11-20 r2 stop ~18 off would have reached 3,502.)
- 8. (rev 11-23) No entry may be live from 2 h before to 30 min after a high-impact US release (FOMC/minutes, CPI, PCE, NFP, GDP, retail sales, ISM, Fed chair); sentiment, PMI flash and regional surveys do not cancel orders. The EA ignores "place after" notes, so a plan meant for after a release posts no entry and the next revision posts it; morning plans cancel 2 h before the release. (11-09 r1 B, 11-17 r1 A -30.15; 11-20 r1 A cancelled 20:00 for UMich final, which came in line, then price fell ~50 past the entry.)
